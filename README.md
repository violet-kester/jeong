### 📖 What it is

An open source journal you read as a timeline instead of a list.

- **Zoom** from a single day out to your whole life
- **Capture** writing, photos, and voice
- **Talk** by recording a quick voice note or chatting with a voice agent that helps you remember

### 🔒 Private by design

- Encrypted as fully as possible
- Your data stays yours
- Shared only with your connections
