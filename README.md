<h1>Jeong</h1>

**Your life on a timeline.**

*Jeong* is a Korean word for the deep, sticky bond that builds up between people over time.<br>
It grows between family, friends, neighbors, acquaintances, strangers, and even rivals.<br>
It can't be rushed. Like snow, it accumulates quietly over shared meals, shared umbrellas, and the stairs you pass each other on every day.<br>
Koreans speak of *miun jeong* and *goun jeong*, the *jeong* of hard times and of sweet ones, and both bring people closer.
